Frequently Asked Questions

How can I submit a news tip?

Email our newsroom at news@abbaytv.et or call +251-11-123-4568. You can also use our contact form and select "News Tip" as the category.

How do I apply for a job?

Visit our Jobs page to see current openings. Send your CV and cover letter to careers@abbaytv.et with the job title in the subject line.

Can I advertise on ABBAY TV?

Yes! Contact our partnerships team at partnerships@abbaytv.et for our media kit and advertising rates.

How do I report a technical issue?

Email tech@abbaytv.et with details about the issue, including screenshots if possible. For urgent issues during live broadcasts, call +251-11-123-4569.
